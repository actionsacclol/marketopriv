## What the winners share

1. **A specific story, not a topic.** The top reels each tell one event with a named actor, exact times and a twist ("six minutes later… one minute later…"). The losers are generic ("a joke coin hit $2.5B") or stale (a story from a year earlier).
2. **Fresh.** The 107K reel covered something from the day before. The two flops on Bonded were old stories. Newsy plus specific beats evergreen plus vague.
3. **Numbers in the hook, then receipts.** The caption is a numbered timeline with UTC timestamps, wallet amounts and named sources (Lookonchain, Decrypt, Bubblemaps, GeckoTerminal). That makes it save-worthy and gives the "read the whole caption" crowd a reason to stay.
4. **45–55 seconds, not 10.** Every winner runs roughly 45–55s, with one sentence per beat and many beats. Our current presets run 9–12s.
5. **One visual metaphor per beat.** Sable: a coin that splits in half on "half", a line chart that spikes on "$15M", a QR card, a wallet-flow diagram, the wallet balance card. Bonded: a big glowing number, a numbered "THE MACHINE" step list, a chart. The screen always shows the thing the sentence says.
6. **Kinetic text, word by word.** Headlines reveal one word at a time (Bonded greys out the words still to come, karaoke-style), timed like narration. Both have audio tracks, and the pacing suggests a voiceover. I couldn't confirm by listening.
7. **A consistent frame.** A small dateline kicker at the top on every beat (`EVENT · DATE · TIME UTC` / `PUMP.FUN · ON CHAIN · 27 OCT 2026`). It reads like a news desk and makes every reel recognisable.
8. **Seamless loop.** Sable's last frame returns to the exact opening shot, so the reel replays without a visible cut, which pads watch time.
9. **Frame 0 is a clean "poster", not a wall of text.** Sable's opening frame is a centred hero object plus the dateline, with the first words appearing within about 1s. The hook lands in the first ~1.5s, but the very first frame is not crammed.
10. **The CTA drives comments.** "Comment BONDED/FACTORY/HOW" took comment counts from ~10 to 63–216. The keyword reel had 216 comments on 1,286 likes. The two accounts cross-promote: Sable sends people to Bonded.

## Two visual systems

- **Sable, light "keynote":** white/grey studio background with soft blur blobs, black and grey type, a 3D hero object, and switches to a dark scene for "reveal" beats. Minimal: two or three elements on screen at once.
- **Bonded, dark "terminal desk":** near-black with a faint grid, a centred vignette, white headline with grey upcoming words, neon green accent for numbers and charts, and red for the bad step. Close to Krypt's look already.

## What we should not copy

- Memecoin "receipts" and P&L-style hooks ("$100 → $146K"). Our rules: no profit claims, nothing that reads like a trade call.
- Pump.fun referral ads and "Ad" posts.
- Screenshots of other people's posts as the content. Since Apr 2026 Instagram doesn't count those as original (see 02).
