## Facebook ranking for a small or new Page (as of 2026-11-20)

Source labels: **[P]** = official or primary Meta source, **[M]** = reported by several outlets, **[S]** = one blog or outlet. Anything dated before 2025 is marked **(pre-2025, may be outdated)**.

### 1. Ranking signals for Reels and Feed

Meta does not publish how much each signal weighs. All three system cards say the models and their signals "change frequently". So I can only rank signal strength by inference.

**Facebook Reels system card** (updated 2026-06-19) [P] https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-reels/
- **Where reels come from:** accounts you follow, reels similar to ones you engaged with, accounts similar to those, and Instagram cross-app recommendations. A light model cuts the pool to about 10–100 reels, then prediction models score them.
- **What the models predict:** watch time, watching to the end, watching rather than skipping, likes, "see more" / "see less", hide, starting a viewing session, sharing to WhatsApp, sharing via Messenger, and how many people you will share with on Messenger.
- **Input signals:** how many people watched it to the end, skip rate, typical watch time, number of views, the author, reel length, topic match, and "whether the reel is original content".
- **Signals that are not there:** following the author is not a named prediction. Comments appear only as an input ("reels you've … commented on").

**Feed system card** (updated 2026-10-06) [P] https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-feed/
- This covers friends, followed Pages and Groups. It predicts scroll-past, skip, "Interested", opening full screen, outside-link clicks plus time spent on the site, and interacting with friends or groups.
- Inputs include the number of comments, "shared or saved by others", and past video watching over 30 seconds.

**Feed Recommendations card** (updated 2026-06-24) [P] https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-feed-recommendations/
- This is how content from Pages you don't follow reaches you. It draws from public posts and predicts time spent, sharing, clicking "X" (dismiss), likes, private shares on Messenger/WhatsApp, screenshots, and "Show more".

**My inference on strength:** watch time, completion and skips sit at the core of both the card and the monetization metrics. Private shares (DM shares) are modelled separately, down to how many people a reel is sent to. "See less", hide and "Not interested" lower scores for that reel and similar ones right away. Reports are a Community Standards matter, not a ranking signal.

**Recent Meta statements:**
- 2025-10-07 [P]: the Reels engine shows "50% more reels from creators published that day". Saves and "Not Interested" tune recommendations. US video watch time is up more than 20% year on year. https://about.fb.com/news/2025/10/finding-sharing-reels-facebook-just-got-easier-more-fun/
- 2026-10-13 [P]: "Your algorithm" lets users type in their own words what they want more or less of, and recommendations change within the same session. Meta says ranking draws on likes, comments, watching and "thousands of other signals". https://about.fb.com/news/2026/10/introducing-your-algorithm-facebook/
- Q3 2026 earnings (2026-10-21) [M]: Facebook Reels time spent hit an all-time high. The Reels ranking model now looks at twice as much viewing history. https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/

### 2. Facebook changes in 2025–2026

- **Friends tab, "OG Facebook" (2025-03-27)** [M]: a separate feed of friends' posts. Home still carries recommendations, and Meta said it has no plans to stop that. https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/27/facebook-debuts-a-revamped-friends-tab-as-part-of-its-return-to-og-facebook
- **Spam crackdown (2025-04-24)** [P]: accounts using "long, distracting captions, often with an inordinate amount of hashtags", or captions unrelated to the content, "will only have their content shown to their followers". Spam networks that run hundreds of accounts sharing the same content lose reach and monetization. Comments detected as coordinated fake engagement are "seen less". Meta told TechCrunch the crackdown does not target AI content as such [M]. https://about.fb.com/news/2025/04/cracking-down-spammy-content-facebook/
- **All videos become Reels (2025-06-17)** [P]: Reels have "no length or format restrictions", and the Video tab became the Reels tab. The Reels Publishing API still accepts only 3–90 seconds [S]. https://about.fb.com/news/2025/06/making-it-easier-create-videos-facebook/
- **Unoriginal-content crackdown (2025-07-14)** [P]: accounts that repeatedly reuse others' content lose reach on everything they post and lose monetization for a while. Copies are demoted. "Simply stitching together clips or adding your watermark" is not a meaningful change. Avoid "visible third party watermarks". https://creators.facebook.com/blog/combating-unoriginal-content
- **Original creators (2026-03-13)** [P]:
  - "Original" means content "filmed or produced directly by" the Page or profile owner.
  - Minor edits are deprioritized, naming "adding borders, inserting captions, and changing the reel's speed".
  - Remixes and overlays count only with "an on-screen presence… presenting something genuinely new".
  - Meta says views of original Reels about doubled in the second half of 2025.
  - https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/
- **Creator Fast Track (2026-03-18, college edition 2026-11-13)** [P]: pay emphasises "deeper engagement, longer watch time, and qualified views". https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/creator-fast-track-grow-your-audience-earn-money-on-facebook/
- **July 2026** [P]: a full-screen video-first home screen test, international first, US possibly in 2027. Also a free selfie-based "Facebook Verified" badge for real people's profiles, separate from paid Meta Verified. https://about.fb.com/news/2026/07/connecting-real-people-on-facebook/
- **August 2026 feed redesign** [S]: an in-feed "see less" tool and interest-based friend suggestions. https://gcn.com/facebook-overhauls-feed-redesign-five-changes/20667/
- **How much of Feed comes from accounts you don't follow:** the last figure is "more than 20%" (2023, pre-2025, may be outdated) [M]. I found no 2025–2026 figure.

### 3. What Meta officially demotes

**Types of content Meta demotes** (updated 2025-07-02) [P] https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/types-of-content-we-demote/
- Clickbait links
- Engagement bait: explicitly asking for votes, shares, comments, tags or likes. Issue-support posts and real calls to action (missing persons, fundraisers) are exempt. https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/content-distribution-guidelines/engagement-bait/
- Fact-checked misinformation
- Content likely to break the Community Standards
- Borderline content (handled by personal ranking)

**Removed on 2025-07-02** [P] https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/cdgs-changes-corrections/
- Removed: Low Quality Comments, Pages Predicted to be Spam, Inauthentic Sharing, Ad Farms, Domains with Limited Original Content, high "click-gap" links, suspicious virality, and 7 more.
- Removed earlier: Low Quality Videos and Pages Posting Unoriginal Videos in 10/2023 (pre-2025). In 12/2023, high-frequency posts to groups were dropped from Inauthentic Sharing (pre-2025).

**Where those rules live now:** originality is enforced through the 2025–2026 creator policies above. Volume is enforced through the Spam Community Standard, which bans posting or engaging "manually or automatically, at very high frequencies" and posting repetitive content [P]. https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/

**AI slop:** no rule bans AI content as such. Spam behaviour and unoriginal content are what get penalised [P/M]. A CNET report says AI reels are ranked the same as other reels [S].

### 4. Links

- **Test started 2025-12-16** [M]: some non-verified Pages and professional-mode profiles capped at 2 organic link posts a month. Meta Verified lifts the cap. Publisher Pages are exempt. Meta called it a "limited test". https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/17/facebook-is-testing-a-link-posting-limit-for-professional-accounts-and-pages
- **Through 2026** [M/S]: reports say the cap is now enforced more widely, sold through "Meta One" tiers ($49.99 for 8 links up to $499.99 for unlimited). Links to Meta properties are reportedly exempt. https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/10/you-must-now-pay-a-subscription-to-post-more-than-two-links-a-month-on-your-facebook-page/ and https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/tech/facebook-professional-accounts-pay-more-post-links-meta-one
- **Links in comments:** reports conflict. Some say they are not restricted (TechCrunch, Social Media Examiner, 2026-02-05). Others say they count toward the cap. I could not retrieve Meta's help page "About links in organic Facebook Page posts and comments".
- **Reach by format** [P/M]: Meta's best practice is captions "with no links" (2025-07). Reels are the format Meta pushes to reach new people. I found no primary data comparing text, image and Reel reach for Pages. The figures that circulate are vendor estimates [S].

### 5. Groups

- A Page can join Groups and post or comment as the Page if the admins allow it. This is in how-to guides [M], not in a Meta 2025–2026 statement. https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/how-to-set-up-facebook-groups-for-pages/
- The Feed card models "deep/strong intent" to engage with Groups you belong to [P].
- Claims that Groups reach "20–60%" of members are unsourced [S].
- Posting the same thing across many Groups falls under the Spam standard's high-frequency and repetitive rules [P].

### 6. Cross-posting from Instagram

- Meta 2025-07 [P]: "Pages and profiles can share the same content". Unoriginal means reusing *another* creator's work.
- The Reels card lists Instagram cross-app recommendations as a source [P]. Meta's Content Protection tool accepts reels cross-posted from Instagram [M].
- So auto-sharing your own Instagram Reel through Accounts Center is not unoriginal.
- The risk is many accounts or Pages posting the same file. That matches the "spam networks… same content" wording [P].

### 7. Professional mode vs Pages

- Both get professional tools and monetization [P]. https://creators.facebook.com/tools/facebook-pages-and-profiles
- Pages add admin roles, Business Suite and Ads Manager.
- The link test applies to both.
- Recommendation status for profiles shows in Professional Dashboard [P].
- I found no primary source saying one format is ranked better than the other.

### 8. Posting frequency and hashtags

- **Frequency:** Meta's guidance [P] is about originality and storytelling ("avoid super short videos that offer viewers little value"), not cadence. "3–5 posts a week" comes from an agency summary of Meta's Reels Playbook [S]. The same-day freshness boost [P] favours posting steadily over dumping a batch.
- **Hashtags:** Meta says best-performing captions have no links, minimal capitals and "no more than five hashtags" [P, 2025-07]. Too many hashtags means followers-only reach [P, 2025-04].

### 9. Myths that primary sources contradict

1. "Reels max out at 90 seconds." No limit since June 2025. Only the API caps at 90 seconds.
2. "Cross-posting your own Instagram Reel is unoriginal." Meta says the same content may be shared across your own Pages and profiles.
3. "AI content is automatically demoted." Meta says spam behaviour and unoriginality are the targets.
4. "More hashtags means more reach." Meta says five or fewer, relevant ones. Excess can mean followers-only reach.
5. "A watermark protects your originality." Adding your own watermark is not a meaningful change. Third-party watermarks hurt.
6. "Adding captions, borders or a speed change makes a repost original." Meta names exactly these as not enough (March 2026).
7. "The Friends tab ended recommendations." Home still recommends, and Meta said it has no plans to stop.
8. "'Low Quality Comments' / 'Inauthentic Sharing' guidelines demote you." Both were removed in July 2025. Spam and personal ranking now cover that ground.
9. "Likes are the main signal." The Reels card's predictions centre on watch time, completion, skips, private shares and hide / see less.

## Rules for an AI agent posting and engaging on Facebook for a small faceless Page

1. **Only post original Reels.** Use your own screen recordings with your own narration, filmed or produced by the Page. Never re-upload other creators' clips. Captions, borders or a speed change do not count as a change. Export without CapCut, TikTok or other-app watermarks.
2. **Build every Reel for watch time and completion.** Show the tool or result in the first 1–2 seconds, because skip rate and completion are core signals. Tell one complete story. Avoid very short clips with little value.
3. **Keep captions short and relevant.** Under about 150 characters, no link in the body, few capitals, 0–3 hashtags (never more than 5), and always about what the video shows.
4. **Never use engagement bait.** No "comment X", "like if", "share this", "tag a friend" or "vote below". A real, specific question is fine. Aim to earn DM shares and saves, not to ask for them.
5. **Treat outside links as scarce.** Assume no more than 2 link posts a month unless the Page has a paid Meta tier. Put the URL in the Page's About / website field. Treat links in comments as unverified and possibly counted.
6. **No investment hype for the Polymarket bots.** Meta's Fraud standard bans "returns guaranteed or risk-free", past performance used to suggest future gains, "quick returns", and "unrealistic financial reward for unclear or minimal effort" (page last dated 2026-10-29). https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/fraud-scams/ The current "AI trades for you, easy" framing is close to that last line. Show what the bot does and say that losses are possible.
7. **Comment like a person, at a person's pace.** Write unique, specific comments. Never paste the same text across posts. Keep to the per-thread cap and spread activity across the session, since the Spam standard bans very high or repetitive frequency, manual or automated, and coordinated fake engagement is demoted.
8. **Never post the same file from many accounts.** Don't run the same Reel across several Pages or installs. Meta names "spam networks… [sharing] the same content". Pushing identical Krypt Reels from many installs is the riskiest pattern for this product. Reposting the Page's own Instagram Reel to its own Facebook Page is fine.
9. **Use Groups sparingly.** Join a few groups on the topic. Post native content as the Page only where the group's rules allow it. Never post the same post across many groups in one session.
10. **Post steadily, no bursts.** About one Reel a day at most, spaced out, to catch the same-day freshness boost. Before posting, check Professional Dashboard / Support for recommendation-eligibility or originality warnings. If the Page is "at risk", stop and tell the user.

## 1. Format data, 2025–2026

- **Reels are what Meta is pushing.** Meta says views and watch time for *original* Reels roughly doubled in the second half of 2025 compared with a year earlier (Mar 13 2026) [M]. Facebook now shows 25%+ more same-day Reels than in Q3 2025 (Jan 2026) [M]. Reels time spent hit an all-time high in Q3 2026, but that figure comes from secondary earnings write-ups [B]. Since June 2025 every video uploaded to Facebook is a Reel, with no length limit [M].
- **Engagement rate by format, as % of followers** (Socialinsider, about 25M posts from about 139K business Pages, 2025) [D]:
  - Status (text) posts: 0.20%
  - Albums and Reels: 0.18% each (Reels were 0.13% in 2024)
  - Images: 0.15%
  - Links: 0.05%
- **Buffer has photos on top** (2026 medians) [D]: images 5.20%, video 4.84%, text 4.76%, links 4.43%. Photos get about 35% more engagement than text posts.
- **How to read the conflict:** engagement-rate studies measure people who already follow. Reels are the format Facebook recommends to non-followers, which is where new follows come from. So: Reels for growth, photos and text for keeping followers active, links last.
- **Reach has grown:**
  - Metricool: Facebook Reels reach +13% while post reach fell 41% (2025 study, 21M posts) [D].
  - Metricool: total Facebook reach +51% year over year (2026 study, Dec 2025) [D].
- **Link posts are throttled.** Meta has tested limiting non-Meta Verified Pages to 2 link posts per month (from Dec 16 2025). In that test, links in comments count toward the limit; affiliate links are exempt [M, via TechCrunch].

## 2. Reel length and hooks

- **Length** (Socialinsider [D]):
  - 2025: about 60s Reels got the most views; 90–120s got the most engagement.
  - 2026 page: peak at 120–180s, with a sharp drop past 180s.
  - Content under 10 seconds does not earn money [M].
- **Hooks** (Emplifi, Mar 19 2026 [D], sample size not stated):
  - Speech starting within the first 3 seconds: 3-second retention +11.7%, 10-second retention +24.7%.
  - On-screen text: +5%.
  - A person visible early: +3.6%.
  - Vertical video: reach +20.9%, 30-second retention +38.5%.
  - Looping clips of 7s or less: reach +23.6%.
- **Meta's own guidance:** don't use "watch till the end" unless the payoff is real (Mar 30 2026) [M].

## 4. What kills reach

- **Unoriginal content** (Meta, Mar 2026 [M]): duplicates, minor edits, re-uploads, and "low-value changes" such as borders, added captions or speed changes. Also reaction or narration over someone else's footage that adds nothing. Penalty: lower reach, then the Page can become "non-recommendable" and lose monetization.
  - Your own screen recordings of Krypt tools are original.
  - Clipped third-party footage needs real added value.
- **Spam signals** (Apr 2025 [M]): long captions with excessive hashtags, captions unrelated to the content, networks of accounts sharing the same content, coordinated fake engagement.
- **Link posts:** 0.05% engagement rate plus the link cap.
- **Engagement bait and clickbait.**
- **Realistic AI video or audio posted without the AI label:** Meta warns of penalties, unspecified [M].
- **"AI slop" with nothing behind it:** Meta says it doesn't target AI content directly; it acts when AI content comes with spam behaviour [M].

## 5. Realistic numbers

- **Industry average:** Pages with 1–5K followers grew about 23% over 2025 (Socialinsider, existing business Pages) [D]. The same dataset puts average views for small Pages very low (10–60 per post). For most new Pages, almost all growth comes from a few Reels that get recommended.
- **New faceless niche Page posting 1–3 original Reels a day (my estimate):**
  - Day 30: 50–300 followers
  - Day 90: 300–3,000
  - Day 180: 1,000–15,000
  - The spread is driven by whether 1–3 Reels break 100K views.
- **Follows per 1,000 Reel views:** no published benchmark. My estimate: about 1–5 for niche or how-to content, 0.2–1 for broad memes. Calculate it yourself as (new follows ÷ views) × 1,000.
- **Outliers:** one 1M-view Reel can bring 2K–10K follows in a week (my estimate). Meme-network operators report 30K–100K+ in months, but that is unverified and often comes from course sellers [S].

## 6. "Mass growth" playbook (faceless, AI-run, real Chrome)

**Before posting (days 1–7)**
- Fill out the Page fully: category, bio, profile and cover images. Turn on professional tools.
- Warm up gently: browse, react, follow 10–20 niche Pages, and join 5–10 groups whose rules allow sharing tools.
- Read each group's rules and note promo days.
- Prepare 15–20 Reels so the Page can post daily from day 1.
- No links in posts.

**Daily**
- **Reels: 2 a day, up to 3, spaced 3+ hours apart.**
  - Spoken hook (Jarnathan) in the first second. On-screen text throughout. Vertical.
  - Mostly 30–75s; one longer 2–3 min "full walkthrough" a few times a week.
  - Rotate 2–3 named series.
  - Polymarket bots: "AI does the trading, easy setup". Never profits, returns or win rates.
- **One photo or text post a day** for existing followers (meme or question about PC tools/AI).
- **Comments as the Page: 15–25 a day** on recent posts (under 1 hour old) from bigger niche Pages (PC gaming, Windows tips, AI tools, Polymarket news). No links.
  - Write like real people type: about 5 words, lowercase, loose. Examples: "this saved my fps lol", "wait it does that free?".
  - Never paste the same comment twice.
- **Replies:** answer every comment on your own posts in the same short style, at most 2 per commenter thread.
- **Invites:** 30–100 a day to people who reacted, spread through the session.
- **Groups:** 1–2 value posts a day, as a profile where allowed. Different text and visuals each time. Only on promo days where groups require it. A link only if the rules allow it, otherwise "link on the page".

**Weekly**
- One photo-carousel "top 5 free tools" post.
- Share the week's best Reel to Stories.
- Re-cut the best Reel's hook into a new version (new opening and text, not a repost).
- Check Account Status / Support home for any recommendation or monetization flags.
- Swap the 2 weakest groups out for new ones.

**What to measure (per Reel, after 48 hours)**
- Views
- 3-second hold rate
- Average watch time
- Follows per 1,000 views
- Shares per 1,000 views
- Non-follower share of reach

Weekly: net follows, which tactic each follow came from (Reels, invites, groups), and Account Status.

**Drop or double a format**
- After 6–8 posts of a format:
  - **Double** it if follows per 1,000 views are at least 2x the Page median, or it has at least one breakout (at least 5x median views).
  - **Drop** it if it stays under 0.5x the median, or hold rate is under 30%.
- Don't touch the posting cadence while a Reel is spreading.
- If reach falls more than 50% for a week, check Account Status for originality or spam flags before changing content.

## Facebook Page: turning viewers into downloads and customers (research as of 2026-11-20)

How strong each source is: **[O]** official Meta page, **[R]** reported by several outlets, **[V]** vendor or tool blog, **[S]** self-reported (forum posts, practitioners), **[C]** controlled test. I found no controlled data on conversion for a small software Page. Every number for conversion or click rate below is anecdotal or comes from a vendor.

### Key facts first

**1. Organic link cap (the biggest constraint)**
- **What it is:** Some Pages without a subscription can put clickable links in only 2 organic posts or comments a month [R].
- **Dates:**
  - It started 2025-12-16 as a "limited test", and Meta confirmed it to TechCrunch on 2025-12-17. Publisher Pages are excluded.
  - Social Media Today reports (2026-09-17) that it spread to more Pages after Meta One launched on 2026-09-15. Meta made no announcement.
- **Which Pages:** Meta's Help Center article on the limit says it "may not apply to all Pages".
- **Counted:**
  - Links in organic Page posts.
  - Links in comments posted by the Page.
- **Exempt:**
  - Links to Meta apps.
  - Links in ads.
  - Affiliate links through Facebook's affiliate partners.
  - Extra links in the comments of a post that already has a counted link.
- **Over the cap:** Sellers report a "0 links left" counter in the composer, and links posted after that show as plain text. There is no strike [S, via batch52].
- **Reset:** On the 1st of each month.
- **Paying to raise it:**
  - Meta One Advanced ($49.99/month) is the first plan that raises the limit [O]: Meta's newsroom lists "links in organic posts and reels" as an Advanced feature.
  - Blotato reads Meta's Help Center as 8, 20 and unlimited links a month for Advanced, Expert and Max. Essential ($14.99) stays at 2 [V].
  - Other summaries give lower numbers, so the tier counts are not settled.
- **Unconfirmed:** Whether the Page button and the About website field count. Nobody states it either way.
- **What to do:** Check the composer for a link counter. If one is there, the first-comment workaround is gone.

**2. Reel captions**
- I found no source saying a URL typed into a Facebook Reel caption is clickable. All the evidence points to plain text unless you pay.
- Meta One lists separate "links in reels" allowances on paid plans only [O/V].
- Treat Reels as a reach tool, not a click tool.

**3. Links get little feed reach**
- Meta's Widely Viewed Content Report (Q1 and Q2 2026) says 98.7–98.9% of US Feed views had no external link [O].

**4. AI voice label**
- Meta's help page "How to identify AI content" says a label may be required for "realistic-sounding audio" and gives "a reel narrated with a realistic AI-generated voiceover" as an example. It warns of penalties when the label is missing [O; read from a search snippet because the page did not render].
- This matters for ElevenLabs-narrated Reels: switch on the "AI info" label.

### Messenger rules

- **Private replies** [O, Messenger Platform docs]:
  - One message per comment, sent within 7 days of the comment.
  - The 24-hour window opens only once the person replies.
  - Promotional content is allowed only inside that window.
  - The Human Agent tag covers manual replies for 7 days.
  - Bots must answer within 30 seconds. Misusing message tags can restrict sending.
- **Messaging people first:** A Page can send at most 10 new message requests a day. The feature is still rolling out, and the person must be an adult whose settings allow it [O, messenger.com help].
- **Free comment-to-DM:** Meta Business Suite's Inbox automations include keyword auto-replies and comment-to-message templates. They run on desktop and take 5 case-sensitive keywords each [V].
- **ManyChat:** Facebook comment triggers work, but only in blank flows. Quick Automations are Instagram-only [S, moderator].
- **Delivery is the weak point:**
  - One Page reported about 1% open rate on automated DMs versus 45–50% on manual ones. The ManyChat moderator says a Page's first message usually lands in Message Requests or Spam, for every tool [S, 2026-09-17].
  - A reported cap of 750 private replies an hour is unverified [S].
- **What this means for the agent:**
  - The hub drives a browser, not the API.
  - Hand-sent private replies from the Page inbox look like the manual case.
  - Also post a short public reply ("sent!") so the commenter gets a notification and knows to check Message Requests.

### Groups without spamming

- Each group sets its own rules, and many ban self-promotion. Read the rules first, and comment as the Page only where the group allows Pages.
- The answer comes first: 2–3 concrete steps, then "I made a free tool that does this, link if you want". Post the link only if the person asks or the rules allow it.
- Never paste the same text twice. Meta's Spam standard targets repetitive content and posting "manually or automatically, at very high frequencies" [O].
- Meta's April 2025 spam crackdown cuts reach for coordinated fake engagement and engagement farming [O].
- The per-thread cap of 2 already fits.

### Policies that bind the products

- **Fraud and Scams** (Community Standards, change log Oct 2026) [O] bans:
  - returns that are "guaranteed or risk-free";
  - "claiming or referencing successful past performance or returns" to suggest similar future results;
  - "quick returns (such as under 24 hours)";
  - "unrealistic financial reward for unclear or minimal effort";
  - gambling offers "with a guarantee of winning".
- **Watch the "easy" line:** "AI trades for you, easy" must describe setup only. Never pair "easy" with money, win rates, P&L screenshots or backtest returns.
- **Ads (only if boosting):**
  - Crypto exchanges, trading platforms and crypto software need written permission or a licence [O].
  - Gambling covers anything where money is part of both the entry and the prize, and needs authorisation [O].
  - I found no Meta rule specific to prediction markets. Amazon Ads classes them as gambling [R]. Assume the bots need authorisation and don't boost them.
- **Branded content:** Paying creators (the Krypt creator program) means their posts need the Paid Partnership label. Meta's branded content policy page would not load today, so verify the details.
- **Links** (Spam standard) [O]: banned are:
  - "a link that promises one type of content but delivers something substantially different";
  - cloaking, meaning showing Meta different content from what users see;
  - gates that demand a captcha, an ad view or a click before the page appears;
  - like/share-gating, meaning "comment to get it" must not require a follow or share.
- **What to use:** Plain krypt.cc URLs with UTMs. No link shorteners that redirect across domains, and no affiliate redirect chains.

### Measuring

- **UTMs:** `?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=<reel|dm|group|button|pin>&utm_campaign=<tool>`. Facebook's link shim adds `fbclid` but keeps UTMs.
- **Insights:**
  - Post link clicks.
  - Page visits or profile views.
  - "Actions on Page", which includes button clicks.
  - Meta deprecated several Page Insights API metrics by 2026-06-15, so read the dashboard, not the API [O, changelog].
- **Downloads per channel:** Measure on krypt.cc (download events by `utm_medium`). DMs need their own `utm_medium=dm` and the keyword as `utm_content`.

### "Push product" playbook (Facebook)

**Funnel:** Demo or before/after Reel (AI info label on, no URL in the caption) → "comment FPS" → public reply plus a private reply with the UTM link → krypt.cc. The pinned post and the Page button catch profile visitors. Group answers catch active searchers.

**Setup (once):**
- Page button: "Use app", or "Learn more" if "Use app" doesn't fit. Website field filled. Both link to krypt.cc with UTMs.
- Check the composer for a link counter.
- Pin a demo post with link 1 of the month.

**Daily:**
- 1 demo or comparison Reel (respect the post gap).
- Reply privately to every keyword comment within the hour, one message each, plus a public "sent" reply.
- 2–4 Group answers that help first; link only when asked.
- Reply to every comment on your own posts.

**Weekly:**
- Rotate the tool featured (tweaker, cleaner, crosshair, macro, editor).
- Post one comparison with a paid tool, using real, dated prices.
- Spend link 2 of the month on the best-performing demo.
- Review UTM downloads by medium and drop the weakest angle.

**Main risks for an AI agent:**
1. Profit wording on bot posts, which triggers the scams rules. Keep the bots to features and setup only; no "open source" or "free to use".
2. Repetitive or high-frequency comments and DMs: spam restrictions, and the Page lands in Message Requests.
3. A missing AI-voice label.
4. Spending the 2 links blindly, or links going plain text without the agent noticing.
5. Group bans from link drops.
6. Facebook's Terms forbid automated access; the browser-driven agent is a ToS risk in itself.
7. Boosting bot posts without authorisation.

**Facebook Page + owner profile: limits, warm-up and restriction risks for a browser AI agent (researched 2026-11-20)**

Labels: **[O]** = official (Meta Help, Terms, Transparency Center, developer docs). **[S]** = reported by several sources. **[A]** = anecdotal or a single vendor/blog. **Meta publishes no numeric limits for browser actions.** Every volume below is [A]/[S].

### 1. How the Page, the profile and Instagram are tied together
- **New Pages Experience.** A Page is a separate presence that a personal profile "switches into". People get either Facebook access (full or partial control, can switch in and post as the Page) or task access (works only through Business Suite or Ads Manager and cannot switch in on facebook.com) [S]. In the browser the agent must check which identity it is acting as before every comment or like, because acting as the wrong one is a common mistake [A]. Sources: https://about.fb.com/news/2021/01/introducing-the-new-page-experience/ (2021), https://help.simplified.com/en/articles/6555916-how-to-manage-access-to-facebook-page-with-the-new-pages-experience
- **Do restrictions spread?** Strikes count across Facebook and Instagram, but restrictions are applied to the Facebook account. Restrictions "generally apply to profiles" and can extend to Pages that represent an individual. Pages that repeatedly break rules lose recommendations, distribution and monetization [O] (https://transparency.meta.com/enforcement/taking-action/restricting-accounts/, updated 2024-11-12). Users often report a Facebook profile being suspended because a linked Instagram account in the same Accounts Center was disabled, or the reverse [S] (https://communityforums.atmeta.com/questions-discussions-38/my-facebook-account-was-suspended-because-my-linked-instagram-account-was-disabled-please-help-182296). Meta's Accounts Center help pages cover login linking only and do not describe this spreading [O] (https://www.meta.com/help/accounts-center/469011081363524/). A restricted owner profile can lose the ability to manage or appeal for its Page [A]. **Practical point:** @yuhgodev has an active Instagram restriction. Do not put a new Facebook Page in the same Accounts Center as a restricted Instagram account.
- **Where to see status.** Account Status shows a Page's violations and restrictions and offers review requests [O]. The Page Integrity API reports a Page as ok, warning ("may be restricted soon") or restricted [O] (https://developers.facebook.com/docs/pages-api/integrity-webhook). The Support home screen and post-level insights show the risk of recommendation or monetization penalties [O] (https://creators.facebook.com/blog/combating-unoriginal-content, 2025-07-14). The old Account Quality page is now Business Support Home [A].

### 2. Limits, blocks and checkpoints
- **Action blocks.** "We limit how often you can post, comment or do other things in a given amount of time in order to help protect the community from spam" is a real Facebook message [S]. Facebook's help page says blocks follow content flagged as suspicious, or messages and friend requests marked unwanted. It says block length depends on how severe the violation was and on account history, and gives no numbers [O] (https://www.facebook.com/help/116393198446749). Third-party guidance says the usual fix is to cut activity for several days [A] (https://www.ayrshare.com/docs/help-center/technical-support/facebook-or-instagram-account-restricted).
- **Strike ladder [O]:**
  - 1 strike: warning only
  - 2–6 strikes: feature limits, such as no posting in Groups
  - 7 strikes: 1 day with no content creation
  - 8 strikes: 3 days
  - 9 strikes: 7 days
  - 10 or more: 30 days, then the account can be disabled
- **Friend requests:** the 5,000-friend cap is official. There is no published daily cap. Vendor guides say 0 requests in week 1 and 2–10 a day later [A].
- **Reels via the API:** 30 published Reels per Page in a rolling 24 hours [O] (https://developers.facebook.com/docs/video-api/guides/reels-publishing, updated 2026-06-30). There is no published browser cap.
- **Group posts:** no official figure. Suggestions range from 5–8 Groups a day with 10–15 minutes between posts and never the same text twice in one session (lilachbullock.com) to 10–20 a day (RecurPost) [A].
- **Group joins:** suggestions range from 3 a day for a new account to 25 a day [A].
- **Messenger (API route only) [O]:** a Page may reply within 24 hours of the user's last message. A human may reply up to 7 days later using the Human Agent tag, but automated replies are not allowed in that period (https://developers.facebook.com/docs/messenger-platform/policy/policy-overview). Cold messages from a Page are not a normal path.
- **Checkpoints:** Meta may ask for a video selfie or ID when it suspects account takeover or needs to confirm the owner [O] (https://www.meta.com/help/policies/3701579170107659/). Users report suspensions where the selfie is the only appeal route [S]. The optional "Facebook Verified" human badge launched in July 2026 [O/S] (https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/24/facebook-announces-face-scanning-to-confirm-youre-a-human/).

### 3. Automation rules and the API route
- **Facebook Terms 3.2.3 [O]:** you may not "access or collect data from our Products using automated means (without our prior permission)", whether logged in or not (https://www.facebook.com/terms.php, effective 2025-01-01). Meta says Platform APIs are the only allowed programmatic access [O] (https://developers.facebook.com/docs/development/terms-and-policies/automated-data-collection/). **Read strictly, driving facebook.com with an AI agent breaks the Terms.** In practice, enforcement comes from behaviour signals, not from detecting automation.
- **Spam standard [O]:** bans "posting, sharing, engaging with content or creating accounts, Groups, Pages, Events… either manually or automatically, at very high frequencies". Accounts at lower frequencies can still be restricted "when other indicators of Spam (e.g., posting repetitive content) or signals of inauthenticity are present". Deceptive links are also banned (https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/).
- **Inauthentic Behavior standard [O]:** covers multiple accounts, hiding who owns or controls a Page, and inauthentic distribution or engagement (https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/inauthentic-behavior, last dated change 2025-12-11). This matters for Mirror-style accounts that follow and like Krypt's own Page.
- **API route [O]:**
  - Page posts and Reels work through the Graph API, and Reels have the 30-per-24h cap.
  - The Groups API (publish_to_groups) was removed on 2024-04-22, so **Group posting is browser-only** (https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/changelog/version19.0/).
  - The API is the only route the Terms allow. It needs a Meta app and app review, which a downloadable product would have to handle.

### 4. What gets new Pages and profiles restricted
- **Repetitive content:** the same text or link pasted across many Groups, or fast Group posting soon after joining [O] (Spam standard) and [S].
- **Link spam:** many comments with links, and links that don't deliver what they promise [O/S]. New Pages commenting with links is the most-reported trigger [S].
- **Unoriginal content:** reposting others' videos without meaningful changes reduces distribution of everything the Page posts and removes monetization. Stitching clips together or adding a watermark does not count as a change [O] (2025-07-14). **This is a direct risk for the clipping style.**
- **Unwanted messages or friend requests** [O, Help page].
- **A brand-new profile that creates a Page on day 1 and posts right away** [A/S].
- **Login changes:** switching IP, proxy or device often, and new logins from a data-centre IP, lead to checkpoints and selfie checks [S].
- **Fake-looking profiles:** an invented name or second accounts break Terms 3.1 (one account, everyday name) [O]. Name mismatches lead to "fake account" checkpoints [S].
- **AI-written comments:** Meta has no rule against AI text as such. Generic, repeated comments trip spam classifiers and get reported by users [A]. This fits the existing "human-sounding text" standard.

### 5. Does a warm-up help? Fit of the current defaults
Meta never mentions warm-ups. Vendors and forums agree that a slow ramp from a stable device and IP reduces checkpoints [S] (e.g. https://multilogin.com/blog/how-to-warm-up-a-facebook-account/, updated 2026-10-12, which uses no friend requests in week 1 and first Group post in week 4).

**Suggested 7 days for a new Page and its new owner profile:**
- **Day 0 (before the Page exists):** the profile should already be at least 3–7 days old, with a real name, photo and a few likes. Create the Page around day 3 of the profile, not on sign-up day [A].
- **Days 1–3:**
  - Page: complete the profile picture, cover, bio and category. Make no posts.
  - Profile: 15–20 minutes of reading, 5–10 reactions and 1–2 follows or Page likes.
  - Join 1 Group on day 3. Send no friend requests.
- **Days 4–5:**
  - Acting as the Page: 10–15 reactions, 1–2 short comments with no links.
  - Profile: join 1–2 more Groups and only read them.
  - Day 5: first original Reel.
- **Days 6–7:**
  - 1 Reel or post a day.
  - 2–3 comments, 2–3 follows.
  - First Group activity is a comment, not a post.
  - Reply to any inbound Messenger messages.
- **Days 8–14:**
  - Up to 1–2 posts a day.
  - First Group post: original, no link, in 1 Group.
  - Comments up to 5–8 a day.
- **Already-active Page (older than 3 months with a clean status):** start at roughly 70% of the 90+ day row of the table below. Raise volumes by about 20% a week.

**Fit of the app defaults:**
- **Likes, follows and comments:** the day 1–3 numbers (10 likes, 2 follows, 0 comments, 0 posts) and the day 4–7 numbers (20 likes, 4 follows, 3 comments, 1 post) fit, and are slightly high for a profile that is also new.
- **Messages do not fit:** 5 messages a day in days 1–3 and 10 in days 4–7 are wrong for Facebook. A new Page has nobody to message, and unwanted messages are a named block trigger. Set messages to **replies to inbound only, 0 cold messages**, for at least 14 days.
- **The repost on days 4–7:** a share of someone else's content works against the unoriginal-content rule on a new Page. Use 0 reposts until day 14, or only share Krypt's own posts.
- **Groups:** set Group posts to 0 until day 8 or later.
- **Order:** delay Page creation if the profile is brand new.
- **Krypt follow:** the day-one follow of Krypt's Page is fine at a volume of 1.

Space every post 2 hours or more apart. Leave 10–15 minutes or more between Group posts. Link comments should stay at 1 in 10 or fewer even after day 30.

### 7. Hard rules for an AI agent running a Page in a browser
1. Use one real Chrome profile on one stable residential IP. Never log in from a new device or IP and act right away, and never run two Facebook identities in one profile.
2. Before every action, check whether it is being done as the Page or as the profile. Pages comment and like as the Page, and Groups mostly need the profile.
3. Never post the same text or link to more than one Group in a day. Never post in a Group within 48 hours of joining it, and read each Group's rules first.
4. Comments should be short, specific and human-sounding, with no links for the first 30 days. Never repeat a comment.
5. Messenger: reply to inbound messages only. Never send cold messages or friend requests in the first 14 days.
6. Post only original or clearly changed content. A watermark or stitched clips are not enough, and clips from others need real commentary and credit.
7. On any block message ("can't use this feature", "We limit how often…"), stop that action for 72 hours and halve volumes for 7 days. On any checkpoint, selfie or ID request, stop everything and hand it to the human. Never solve verification automatically.
8. Check Account Status and Page Status (Support home) before each run, and skip the run if there is a warning or restriction.
9. Do not link the Page into an Accounts Center that holds a restricted Instagram account. Keep at least two admins with Facebook access on separate devices.
10. Use the Graph API wherever possible (Page posts and Reels, within 30 a day). It is the only route the Terms allow. Groups can only be done in the browser and carry the most risk, so keep them small.

### Patterns across the winners

1. **Original screen recordings win, reposts lose.** Meta (13 Mar 2026) rewards original or transformed Reels and pushes down reposts and stitched clips. Small edits (captions, borders, speed changes) don't count as original. Meta says original Reels views roughly doubled in the second half of 2025. https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators/
2. **Reels reach people who don't follow you; small Pages can break out.** Text on screen in the first 2 seconds matters because many people watch with sound off. https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/facebook-growth-strategy/
3. **Length: about 15-30s for reach, longer for engagement.** Meta removed Reel length limits in 2025, and there is no official best length. https://socialbee.com/blog/how-long-can-a-facebook-reel-be/
4. **Photo and image posts still pay.** In the finance case above they made most of the revenue. Carousels of tips are cheap to make.
5. **Few links in posts.** Since Dec 2025 Meta has tested a cap of two link posts a month for some Pages without Meta Verified. Whether comment links are also limited is disputed between outlets. https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/17/facebook-is-testing-a-link-posting-limit-for-professional-accounts-and-pages
6. **Comment keyword to an automatic reply** (ManyChat-style, e.g. a viewer comments "INFO" and gets the link) is the usual way around link limits.
7. **Groups convert better than Pages for software,** but only through admins or promo threads.
8. **Free tier plus referrals** (Hone) beats hard selling.
9. **Big prediction-market brands buy reach** with paid ads and celebrities rather than organic Pages. Their audience is on X, TikTok and Instagram.
