TikTok For You feed ranking research, as of 2026-11-20

Labels: **[O]** official or primary TikTok source. **[M]** reported by several outlets. **[B]** a single blog or vendor. **(pre-2025)** means it may be outdated.

## 1. Ownership change (2025-2026)
- **TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC** (announced 2026-01-23) now runs TikTok in the US. Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX hold 15% each and ByteDance keeps 19.9%. The venture will "retrain, test, and update the content recommendation algorithm on U.S. user data," hosted in Oracle's US cloud. It also controls trust and safety and moderation. **[O]** https://newsroom.tiktok.com/announcement-from-the-new-tiktok-usds-joint-venture-llc?lang=en
- **New US terms and privacy policy**, about 2026-01-22. They cover data and location; no ranking change was announced. **[M]** https://opentermsarchive.org/en/memos/tiktok-rewrites-all-terms-following-usa-operations-transfer/
- **No public detail on what the retraining changed.** Sen. Markey's letter of 2026-05-29 says USDS has not explained what "retraining" means. **[O, government]** https://www.markey.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/letter_to_tiktok_usds.pdf
- **Creators reported reach and payout drops after the sale.** These reports are unconfirmed. Claims of "3 confirmed algorithm updates in 2026" come from blogs only. **[B]** https://eurweb.com/tiktok-payout-drop/ , https://ttcalculator.net/blog/tiktok-algorithm-changes-2026-roundup/
- **Bottom line:** there is no official evidence that ranking signals changed. Assume the same signal model.

## 2. Signals and how strong each is
- **What TikTok says it ranks on.** It predicts how likely each viewer is to like, share, comment or *skip* a video, combines those into one score, and serves the highest scores. Its inputs:
  - views, likes, shares, skips and finishes, and follows;
  - sound, length, hashtags and posting time/region;
  - device and language, which count for less.
  - **[O]** https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system (page © 2025, undated)
- **Finishing a longer video is a "strong indicator."** **[O] (pre-2025, June 2020)** https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you
- **Follower count is not a direct ranking factor.** Nor are an account's past high-performing videos. **[O] (pre-2025, June 2020)**, same page.
- **Watch time and completion.** A leaked internal doc ("Algo 101", NYT, Dec 2021) gives the score as roughly predicted like × value + predicted comment × value + expected playtime × value + predicted play × value. Watch time is the dominant term. TikTok confirmed the doc was real. **[M] (pre-2025)** https://www.techtimes.com/articles/269024/20211206/tiktok-algo-101-leaked-document-tiktok-secret-algorithm-tiktok.htm
- **Shares and comments.** Both are predicted, scored signals **[O]**. That shares outweigh likes is widely claimed **[M]**, but TikTok publishes no weights.
- **Saves/favorites, searching and watching** shape a viewer's feed **[O, 2025-06-03]** https://newsroom.tiktok.com/more-ways-to-discover-new-content-and-creators-you-love?lang=en
  - That a save is "worth more than a like" is a blog claim **[B]**.
- **Rewatches/replays.** These are among the first signals that personalize a feed **[O] (pre-2025, 2020)**. No weight is published.
- **"Not interested", Manage Topics and keyword filters** are explicit negative feedback **[O, 2025-06-03]**.
- **Skips** are a predicted negative signal **[O]**. The "first 2-3 seconds decide" claim is **[M]** advice only. TikTok gives no number.
- **Profile visits, and follows from a video:** follows are a listed signal **[O]**. Profile visits are not named by TikTok **[B]**.
- **Diversity rule.** Near-identical videos in a row, such as ones using the same sound, get swapped out for variety, and local creators are mixed in **[O]**.

## 3. Batch testing and view tiers
- **What TikTok says:** only that it pulls a candidate pool of moderated videos and ranks them **[O]**.
- **What it does not say:** it has never published batch sizes. The "first 200-500 viewers, then 1k, then 10k" model traces back to Chinese explainers about Douyin's "traffic pool." **[B]** https://cut.pro/en/blog/tiktok-stuck-at-200-views-2026
- **Takeaway:** a small initial exposure that expands on strong signals is plausible and consistent with how TikTok describes the system. The exact tiers are folklore.
- **Unverified 2026 claim:** that the first test now goes to followers first **[B]**.

## 4. When a video can't be in the For You feed
**Current Community Guidelines.** The Integrity & Authenticity page shows released 2025-08-25 / effective 2025-09-24 per that fetch. A New York AG report dated the update 2026-09-24 and I couldn't reconcile the two; treat the current page as the authority. https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity
- **Unoriginal or reused material** is ineligible: "unoriginal or reused material without anything new" **[O]**.
- **Bought engagement and automation:** "We strictly prohibit automation tools, scripts, or other tricks designed to bypass our systems." Selling engagement is banned. Fake likes and follows are removed. Content that coaxes likes, follows or gifts is ineligible **[O]**. **This matters most for an AI-run account.**
- **QR codes.** The April 2024 version made videos with QR codes ineligible **[O, pre-2025]**. In Aug 2025 TikTok folded the long ineligibility list into the individual policy sections **[M]** https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/15/tiktoks-new-guidelines-add-subtle-changes-for-live-creators-ai-content-and-more/ . I could not confirm that QR wording is still on the current page, so treat QR codes as still risky.
- **Misinformation** that "may cause moderate harm" is ineligible **[O]**. This is relevant to trading-bot claims.
- **Not suitable for a broad audience:** ineligible. Accounts aged under 16 are ineligible **[O]**.
- **Account-wide:** repeated ineligible posts can make the whole account ineligible for a while **[O, pre-2025, Apr 2024]** https://newsroom.tiktok.com/cgupdate
- **Being off the For You feed is not a strike.** Search and the profile still work **[O]** https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards
- **Low quality (Creator Rewards definition).** Creator Academy calls content unoriginal if it has "minimal original input or edits." It lists slide videos, split screens, meaningless reactions and low-quality images as low quality **[O]** https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/creator-rewards-program
- **Watermarks from other apps:** widely cited as a trigger **[M]**. I found no current official wording.
- **Pre-checks before posting:**
  - TikTok Studio on the web has **Content Check Lite**, which checks For You eligibility before you post **[M]** https://www.engadget.com/social-media/tiktok-content-check-tells-creators-if-their-videos-will-be-blocked-from-for-you-pages-110015168.html
  - The reason a video is ineligible shows in its analytics, and it can be appealed **[M]**.

## 5. AI-generated content
- **What must be labelled:** "AI-generated or significantly edited content that shows realistic-looking scenes or people." Unlabelled content may be removed, restricted, or labelled by TikTok **[O, current CG]**.
- **Auto-labelling** uses C2PA Content Credentials (since May 2024) plus TikTok's own detection models. Over 1.3B videos are labelled. Labels can't be removed by creators **[O]**.
- **Invisible watermarks** are being tested on TikTok-made content and on C2PA uploads **[O, 2025-11-19]** https://newsroom.tiktok.com/more-ways-to-spot-shape-and-understand-ai-content?lang=en
- **Reach effect:**
  - TikTok states no ranking penalty for labelled AI content.
  - Viewers get a "Manage topics" slider for how much AI content they see. It was announced 2025-11-19 and was still in testing in March 2026 **[O]**. The effect is reach loss among viewers who turn AI content down.
- **TTS narration over real screen recordings** is not "realistic scenes or people," so a label is likely not required. That is my inference, not stated by TikTok.

## 6. Video length and Creator Rewards
- **Creator Rewards needs videos over 1 minute.** They must be original and high quality. Photo Mode is excluded, as are ads and sponsored posts **[O]**.
- **Account requirements:** ≥10k followers, ≥100k views in 30 days, age 18+ **[M]**.
- **What the payout weighs:** originality, play duration (watch time and finish rate), search value, and audience engagement **[O, pre-2025, 2024-03-18]** https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program
- **Performance by length (third-party data):**
  - Over 1 minute reached ~70% more people than 10-30s (Buffer 2025) **[M]** https://buffer.com/resources/longer-tiktoks-get-more-views-data/
  - ~30s has the best engagement rate; 2-3 min gets the most views (Socialinsider, Jul 2026, ~6M videos) **[B]** https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/how-long-are-tiktok-videos/
  - 30-90s has the highest interaction (Fanpage Karma) **[B]**
- **No clean winner.** Completion is easier on short videos; total watch time is higher on long ones.

## 7. Search (TikTok SEO)
- **Official:** captions, sounds and hashtags are video information used for ranking **[O]**. Search is part of Creator Rewards ("search value", Creator Search Insights) **[O, 2024]**.
- **Reported, not documented:** spoken words are transcribed, on-screen text is read by OCR, and the caption is indexed **[M]**. TikTok has no public doc on how much each counts.
- **"First 50 caption characters weigh most"** **[B]**, no source.
- **Hashtag count:** no official limit or optimum. "3-5" is blog advice **[B]**.

## 8. Audio
- **Sound is a ranking input** **[O]**. The diversity rule spreads out videos that use the same sound **[O]**.
- **Business accounts** only see the Commercial Music Library, which is pre-cleared and royalty-free **[O]** https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article?aid=10005905
- **No official claim** that trending sounds, original audio or library tracks get more reach.

## 9. Posting frequency, timing, deleting and reposting
- **Frequency:** "1-4 posts a day" is credited to old Creator Portal advice **[M] (pre-2025, ~2021)**. Buffer's 11M-post data: posting more raises the odds of a hit but not the average performance **[M]** https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/
- **Timing:** posting time and region are inputs **[O]**. Use the follower-activity view in analytics. No official best hour.
- **Deleting and reposting:**
  - TikTok doesn't recommend duplicate content **[O, pre-2025]**.
  - Spam and attempts to "artificially boost traffic" are excluded **[O]**.
  - A named "repost penalty" is not official. Delete-and-repost loops can be treated as spam **[B]**.

## 10. Account type, formats, text
- **Personal vs business reach:** no official difference. Blogs disagree **[B]**. The real difference is the music library, plus Creator Rewards requires a personal account **[M]**.
- **Photo mode / carousels:**
  - TikTok's own figures: 2.9x comments, 1.9x likes vs video **[M, pre-2025]** https://www.modernretail.co/technology/tiktok-is-pushing-brands-to-post-photos-instead-of-videos/
  - Fanpage Karma 2025: ~3% more reach than video **[B]**.
  - Buffer 2026: video gets 77% more engagement **[B]** https://buffer.com/resources/data-best-content-format-social-media/
  - Photo posts are excluded from Creator Rewards **[O]**.
- **Text overlays:** no ranking rule. Keep text inside the safe zones TikTok publishes for ads and away from the UI on the right and bottom **[M]**. Readable text helps watch time and indexing **[M]**.

## 11. Myths contradicted by primary sources
1. **"You need followers to reach the For You feed"** — follower count is not a direct factor **[O]**.
2. **"Fixed 200/500/1k batches"** — never published by TikTok. It comes from Douyin lore.
3. **"Being off the For You feed means shadowban or violation"** — TikTok says it doesn't necessarily mean a rule was broken.
4. **"AI-labelled content is suppressed"** — no stated penalty. Only viewer-chosen AI filtering.
5. **"Hashtags like #fyp get you on the FYP"** — hashtags only describe content **[O]**.
6. **"The US sale reset the algorithm in Jan 2026"** — only retraining was announced. No ranking change was published.

## Rules for an AI agent posting on TikTok for a small faceless account
1. **Don't look like automation.**
   - TikTok "strictly prohibit[s] automation tools, scripts, or other tricks designed to bypass our systems."
   - Act at human pace with varied timing: a few comments per session, no bursts, no repeated templates.
   - Never buy or trade engagement, and never ask for likes or follows.
2. **Make every upload original.**
   - Record your own screen, add narration and on-screen text edits.
   - No reused clips, no watermarks from CapCut, Instagram or other apps, no QR codes, no slideshow-only videos.
   - Run Content Check Lite in TikTok Studio (web) before publishing when it's offered.
3. **Hook in the first 1-2 seconds**, by showing the result (the tool working, or the bot's P&L screen). Skips are a scored negative, and completion is the strongest positive.
4. **Pick length by goal.**
   - 20-45s demos for completion.
   - Over 61s only when the content justifies it, which it must for Creator Rewards later.
   - Judge by average watch % in analytics, not by views.
5. **Write for search.** Say the main keyword in the first sentence of the TTS script. Show it as on-screen text in the first seconds. Repeat it at the start of the caption, plus 3-5 specific hashtags.
6. **Keep trading-bot claims factual.**
   - No promised returns. Show real paper or live results with a risk note.
   - Misleading financial claims can make videos ineligible or removed.
   - Use text big, high-contrast and inside the safe zones.
7. **Label AI only when it applies.** Turn on the AI-generated label if visuals show realistic AI-made people or scenes. TTS over real screen recordings likely doesn't need it.
8. **Post once or twice a day at most, hours apart**, at the follower-activity peaks shown in analytics. Never post the same file twice or across many accounts. Re-cut with a new hook instead of delete-and-repost.
9. **Stay on a personal account** unless commercial sounds or ads are needed. Use original audio or the voice track; trending sounds give no proven boost.
10. **Watch for ineligibility.**
    - Check each post's analytics for a For You "not eligible" notice.
    - Appeal real false flags.
    - If two or more are flagged in 30 days, stop posting and review. Repeated ineligible posts can make the whole account ineligible for a time.

# Fastest follower growth for a faceless, AI-run TikTok account (as of 2026-11-20)

How strong each source is: **[P]** TikTok's own statement or policy. **[L]** a large dataset measured by a vendor (100k+ posts). **[A]** an academic study. **[V]** a vendor blog or marketing claim. **[S]** a self-reported case or anecdote. **[E]** my own estimate from the sources, not measured.

## Two risks to settle first
- **Automation ban [P].** TikTok's Integrity & Authenticity guidelines (released 2026-08-25, in force 2026-09-24) say: "We strictly prohibit automation tools, scripts, or other tricks designed to bypass our systems." Breaking it can mean removal, a ban, or a restriction that keeps the account out of search and the For You feed (FYF). https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity/
  - An AI agent driving TikTok web is therefore **grey at best**. It needs human pacing, human-volume actions, and no bursts.
  - Posting from web vs phone: there is no controlled data, only vendor and forum anecdotes of web uploads getting low views [S] (https://www.tokportal.com/post/tiktok-website-vs-app-growth-moves-that-differ). **Test it.**
- **Polymarket content is grey.** The Community Guidelines reportedly ban marketing "gambling or gambling-like activities." TikTok's July 2026 ad policy lets prediction-market ads run only if they are framed as trading, not betting [V].
  - Use tool and bot language. Never say "bet", odds-to-win or payouts, and never make profit claims.
  - Sources: https://sccgmanagement.com/sccg-articles/2026/09/22/tiktoks-ad-policy-ties-prediction-market-eligibility-to-trading-not-betting-language/ and https://www.covers.com/industry/tiktok-reaffirms-ban-on-gambling-sweepstakes-promotions-nov-6-2026

## How TikTok ranks videos (confirmed)
- **[P, 2020-06-18]** Signals are what viewers do, video info (caption, sound, hashtags) and device. Finishing a longer video is a strong signal.
- Follower count and past hit videos are **not direct factors**.
- TikTok does not recommend duplicated content or spam. https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you
- **[P, 2026]** A video is left out of the FYF if it is "unoriginal or reused material without anything new," or if it "tries to manipulate people into… inflating likes or follows."
  - That means "follow for part 2" and "like + follow to win" put the video at risk of losing FYF reach.
- **[P, May 2024]** Posting against these rules repeatedly can make the whole account temporarily ineligible for the FYF. https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/04/18/tiktok-moderation-problematic-content-accounts-on-for-you-page/amp/

## Tactics ranked by expected follower gain

1. **Original, niche-consistent videos people search for — safe.**
   - Evidence:
     - Creator Rewards favors "high-quality, original content that is over a minute long" and scores "search value" [P, 2024-03]: https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program
     - TikTok search volume grew more than 40% year on year [V citing TikTok].
     - Buffer niche case: one on-niche video got 70K views and 757 follows, about **11 follows per 1,000 views**. An off-niche viral video converted poorly [S]: https://buffer.com/library/how-to-get-followers-on-tiktok/
   - How to do it:
     - Name the video after the exact search: "fix fps drops windows 11", "best free crosshair overlay", "free video editor no watermark", "polymarket bot setup".
     - Put that phrase in the on-screen text, the spoken line and the caption, plus 3-5 hashtags.
     - Real screen recordings of the tools count as original footage. They are the strongest defence against both the "AI slop" and the "unoriginal" labels.

2. **Hook in the first 1-2 seconds — safe.**
   - Evidence:
     - TikTok's ad creative guide says over 63% of the ads with the highest click-through rates show the key message in the first 3 seconds [P, ads only; via https://influencermarketinghub.com/viral-tiktok-hooks-examples/].
     - A preprint of 46,605 hooks found meta/community humor and fast, CTA-heavy openers predict engagement best [A, preprint]: https://sciety.org/articles/activity/10.31235/osf.io/rj2mz_v1
     - Semrush, 500 trending videos [V]: https://www.semrush.com/blog/hooks-of-viral-tiktoks/
   - How to do it: show the result first (FPS counter jumping, drive space freed, bot dashboard) with a 4-7 word text hook on frame 1.

3. **Posting volume — safe if ramped up slowly; grey if it jumps suddenly.**
   - Buffer, 11.4M posts from 150K accounts, 2025-10-08 [L] (https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/). Views per post compared with posting once a week:
     - 2-5 posts a week: +17%
     - 6-10 a week: +29%
     - 11+ a week: +34%
   - The **median stays flat at about 500 views** at every frequency. What grows is the 90th percentile: 3,722 views at 1 a week vs 14,401 at 11+ a week.
   - So volume buys more chances at a breakout, not a better typical post.
   - There is no data on 1 vs 3+ posts a day. The often-quoted "TikTok says 1-4 a day" never traces back to a TikTok page [V].
   - Ramp-up limits are not published. Sudden volume jumps on a new account are a widely reported practitioner risk [E].

4. **Numbered series and "Part 2" follow-ups to winners — safe.** Avoid "follow for part 2" in the caption; "part 2 is on my page" is fine.
   - Buffer cites a nearly 300-part series with 5M+ views [S].
   - Mechanism: a "Part N" label sends viewers to the profile, where follows happen.
   - Profile playlists need about **10K followers** [V] (https://www.kapwing.com/resources/tiktok-playlists/). Until then, pin Part 1 and use "Part N" in titles.

5. **Length — safe.**
   - Buffer, 1M+ posts [L]: videos over 60s got 43.2% more reach than 30-60s, 70.3% more than 10-30s, and 95.7% more than 5-10s.
   - Socialinsider [L/V]: 2-minute videos had a median of about 11K views, roughly 11x a 15-30s video. Engagement rate peaks around 30s. https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/how-long-are-tiktok-videos/
   - Fan Page Karma, 32K videos: engagement peaks at 30-90s, and completion falls as length rises [L/V].
   - Use:
     - 45-90s for tool walkthroughs and setups (this is also the Creator Rewards path).
     - 7-15s loops for single "watch this" before/after results.
     - 1-3 min only for tutorials that already win.

6. **Video replies to comments — safe.**
   - This is an official feature [P] (https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-gb/product-tutorial-reply-to-comments-with-video).
   - There is no measured lift. But each reply is a new video that answers a real question, which keeps the feed fed with search-shaped content [E].

7. **Stitches and duets of big niche videos — safe if they add something.**
   - No data, only Buffer anecdotes [S].
   - Reacting without adding anything counts as "unoriginal."
   - For a faceless account, the stitch should add a screen recording, e.g. "here's the free version."

8. **Early comments on other creators' videos — safe at human volume; grey at scale.**
   - No controlled data. In the only real test (RJI), 7 comments drew 2 replies [S]. https://rjionline.org/news/how-to-engage-impactfully-in-the-comment-section
   - Mechanism: early top comments get seen, and a funny or useful one leads people to the profile [E].

9. **On-screen text and captions — safe, supporting.**
   - The direction is consistent but the size of the effect is not reliable: a 12-40% watch-time lift [V]. https://pctechmag.com/2026/05/why-captions-and-on-screen-text-affect-tiktoks-watch-time-algorithm/
   - They are needed anyway for search.

10. **Trending sounds and formats — safe, small effect for this niche.**
    - The evidence is mostly TikTok case studies [P, single song].
    - Business accounts can only use the Commercial Music Library (about 1M tracks) [P]. https://newsroom.tiktok.com/commercial-music-library
    - Use trending formats, which are free, more than trending songs, and keep music under the voice.

11. **Cross-posting from Instagram or YouTube — safe if it's your own clean file; restricted if it's watermarked or someone else's.**
    - "Reused material without anything new" loses FYF eligibility [P].
    - TikTok's text does not mention watermarks. Still, upload the clean original export and never another platform's download.

12. **AI voice / TTS faceless formats — safe if labeled and the visuals are original.**
    - AI content is everywhere:
      - Kapwing (2026-06) found 59% of a fresh account's first 500 FYF videos were AI slop [V, single account]: https://kapwing.com/resources/the-tiktok-ai-slop-report/
      - AI Forensics found 354 "agentic AI accounts" with 43K posts and 4.5B views in one month (report 2025-12-03) [A/NGO]: https://aiforensics.org/work/agentic-ai-accounts
    - Pushback:
      - Since 2025-11 viewers can turn AI content down with a slider, and TikTok adds invisible watermarks [P]: https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/18/tiktok-now-lets-you-choose-how-much-ai-generated-content-you-want-to-see/
      - Labels are required for realistic AI scenes [P].
    - TU Delft found labeled videos had *higher* median views [A]. https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_629244e4-d687-4934-9a0c-e2ce0d95d851?preview=1
    - Verdict: TTS narration over real screen recordings still grows. AI-generated visuals with an AI script are what sliders and slop filters hit.

13. **LIVE — not available yet.** It needs 1,000 followers. Sources disagree on the minimum age (16+ or 18+) [V]. https://www.demandsage.com/followers-needed-for-tiktok-live/

14. **Collabs — limited for a faceless account.** No data. Duets and stitches are the practical version.

15. **Giveaways — grey.** "Follow + like to win" fits the FYF engagement-manipulation exclusion [P]. Give a free download with no follow requirement instead.

16. **Follow/unfollow — gets restricted.**
    - Community-reported limits are about 30 follows an hour and 200 a day, lower for new accounts [S]. https://exolyt.com/guides/how-to-fix-you-are-following-too-fast-on-tiktok
    - Low yield, and it trips action blocks and the automation clause.

17. **Engagement pods — get restricted.** TikTok disrupted 30 engagement-manipulation networks, removing 76K accounts and 4.8M posts in one quarter [P via https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-us-shares-q2-account-enforcement-data/833907/].

18. **Bought followers — get restricted.** In Q2 2025 TikTok removed 781.4M fake US followers and blocked 403.2M fake follow requests [P via https://www.contentgrip.com/tiktok-fake-account-crackdown/]. Fake followers also crash the views-per-follower ratio.

**What kills reach:**
- reposting or unoriginal material [P]
- engagement bait [P]
- automation and bursts [P]
- realistic AI visuals without a label [P]
- watermarks from other apps [V]
- gambling wording [V]
- off-niche viral videos, which bring views but few follows [S]
- repetitive, LLM-sounding comments (spam clause [P]; no detection data)
- sudden volume jumps [E]

## Realistic numbers
- **Views per post:** a median of about 500 for typical small and mid accounts [L]. Accounts under 5K followers get about 43 views per 100 followers per video [L/V]. https://www.rivaliq.com/blog/tiktok-benchmark-report/
- **Follows per 1,000 views:**
  - About 1-3 for broad or entertainment views [E].
  - About 5-15 for niche how-to content that solves a problem [S, Buffer case about 11].
  - A video that "won" but converts under 2 per 1,000 is in the wrong niche or has a weak profile.
- **Engagement rate by views:**
  - Averages are 3.4% (Dash H1 2026) [L/V] and 4.2% (Socialinsider 2025) [L/V]: https://www.dashsocial.com/social-media-benchmarks/tiktok and https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/tiktok
  - Good is 5% or more; strong is 8% or more [E].
- **Completion [E]:**
  - 7-15s loops: aim for 60-70%+ watched in full, with average watch time above 100% of the length (replays).
  - 30-60s: aim for 35-50%.
  - 1-3 min: aim for 25-35% average watched.
  - Above about 50% completion is commonly cited as the boost line [V].
- **Follower growth for a new faceless niche account at 2-3 posts a day [E, from the sources]:**

| Day | Typical | Top quartile | Outlier |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 0-300 | 300-1,500 | one breakout, 5-20K |
| 90 | 300-2,000 | 2-10K | 50-100K [V, self-reported] |
| 180 | 1-8K | 10-30K | 100K+ |

  - Buffer: "most creators reach 1,000+ in 3-6 months" [V, unsourced].
  - A faceless YouTube Shorts channel took 157 days to reach 1K subscribers [S]: https://kapwing.com/resources/we-grew-a-faceless-youtube-channel-to-1000-subscribers
  - Vendor claims of 10K in 30-60 days are best cases [V]: https://clippie.ai/blog/grow-faceless-tiktok-0-to-10k-followers-ai-2026
- **Outliers:** about 1 in 10 posts carries the account. The 90th percentile is 7-31x the median [L].

## "Mass growth" playbook (TikTok, faceless, AI agent in Chrome)

**Before posting (days 1-5, warm-up)**
- Profile:
  - The name contains the niche keyword ("Krypt | free PC tools").
  - A one-line bio saying what followers get.
  - The link only once the account allows it.
- **Business account:** only if needed. It limits the account to Commercial Music Library sounds; a personal/creator account keeps full sound access.
- Daily warm-up:
  - Watch niche videos for 15-25 minutes: search the niche keywords and watch most videos to the end.
  - Like 10-20 and follow 5-10 niche creators.
  - Leave 3-5 short comments.
- Prepare 10 videos before the first post. Note the AI label rule: label realistic AI visuals, and TTS over screen recordings still gets "AI-generated" turned on if the toggle applies.

**Daily (from day 6)**
- **Posts:**
  - Week 1: 1 a day. Weeks 2-3: 2 a day. Week 4 on: 2-3 a day.
  - Space them at least 3 hours apart, never more than one step up a week, and never a 5+ batch.
- **Mix per day:**
  - 1 search-led walkthrough, 45-90s.
  - 1 short result loop, 7-15s.
  - Optionally 1 "Part N" of the latest winner, or a video reply to the best comment.
- **Format:**
  - Hook on frame 1: result plus a 4-7 word text.
  - Burned-in captions and the keyword spoken in the first line.
  - Caption: the searched phrase plus 3-5 hashtags.
  - A soft CTA: "part 2 on my page" or "comment 'link'". Never "follow for part 2."
- **Your own comments:**
  - Reply to every comment within the first 60 minutes after posting. That is the most active window [E].
  - Reply with a video to 1 good question a day.
  - At most 2 replies per thread.
- **Comments on others: 10-20 a day, in 3-4 sessions.**
  - Targets: niche videos (PC tweaks, gaming setup, AI tools, Polymarket/trading tools) **posted in the last 15-60 minutes** by creators with 10K-500K followers. Their comment sections are still small, so an early comment can rise to the top.
  - Skip videos that are already huge, have 1K+ comments, or are older than 6 hours.
  - Comments are about 5 words, loose and lowercase-ish:
    - "wait this actually worked"
    - "my fps went up lol"
    - "which cleaner is that"
  - No links, no self-promotion, never the same comment twice in a day, and no LLM tells (no em dashes, no "Great tip!", no full sentences with semicolons).

**Weekly**
- Read TikTok Studio for each video:
  - views
  - average watch time and % watched in full
  - retention at 2s and 3s
  - search % in traffic sources
  - **follows per 1,000 views**
- Turn the top 2 videos into a series (Part 2 and Part 3 within 48 hours) and one longer version.
- Stitch or duet 2-3 big niche videos, adding a screen recording.
- Check the in-app Account check for any FYF restriction before raising volume.

**What to measure:** follows per 1,000 views (main KPI), 3s hold, completion, engagement % of views, search share, profile visits per 1,000 views.

**Drop or double [E]:**
- **Double** a format with 2 or more of its last 5 posts above 3x the account median, OR more than 8 follows per 1,000 views. Make 3 variants plus a Part 2 within 48 hours.
- **Fix the hook** if the 3s hold is under 50% (completion fine but the start is weak).
- **Fix the profile or niche** if views are high but under 2 follows per 1,000 views.
- **Drop** a format after 6-8 posts with no post above 2x the median.
- **Stop scaling** at any FYF restriction notice, a sudden drop of more than 70% across all posts, or action blocks. Go back to 1 post a day, stop comments and follows for 48-72 hours, then step back up.

Limits of this research: no controlled data exists for comments per day, video replies, stitches, 1 vs 3 posts a day, or web vs app posting. Treat those as tests to run.

## TikTok viewers to customers for a small faceless, AI-run software account (research as of 2026-11-20)

**Evidence labels:** **Official** = TikTok's own pages. **Commissioned** = studies TikTok or a vendor paid for. **Vendor** = marketing blogs and tool companies. **Self-reported** = one creator's anecdote. **None** = no data found.

I found no controlled study of TikTok views turning into downloads for software. Every number below is weak, so treat the ranking as reasoned judgment, not measurement.

### Ranked tactics (by expected customers)

**1. Clickable bio link on a Business account, pointing to one tagged landing page.** It is the only one-tap path off TikTok, and everything else feeds it.
- **Personal accounts:** need 1,000 followers. This is the consensus of third-party guides (Buffer; Stan, updated 2026-09-16). I found no official TikTok page stating it.
- **Business accounts:** most guides say the link field appears right away, with no follower minimum (Bitly; Stan 2026-09). Others say an account must be *registered/verified* as a business (18+, documents, supported market) (Technobezz). The sources conflict. Switch to Business and check Edit profile > Website.
- **Trade-off:** Business accounts lose most trending music and get only the Commercial Music Library.
- **Other limits:** the link can only be set in the mobile app. TikTok removed the separate Instagram/YouTube link fields and does not support links that open other social apps (Social Media Today).
- **Link-in-bio pages (Linktree and similar):** they add a tap. For a single product, link straight to a krypt.cc page with one big download button per tool.
- **Click-through numbers:** vendor figures range from 1–3% to 6–12% of profile visitors, with no stated method (ttcalculator, Rebrandly). Evidence: vendor, weak.

**2. Search-intent demo videos** ("best free FPS booster", "fix stutter Windows 11", "remove bloatware").
- Adobe's 2026 survey (807 US consumers): 49% use TikTok as a search engine, up from 41% in 2024. Of those, 61% want video tutorials and 45% product reviews. Only 7% rely on TikTok more than Google. Evidence: vendor survey, moderate.
- Put the exact phrase in the spoken line, the on-screen text and the caption. These videos keep getting found for months, which suits a small account.

**3. Before/after and screen demos** (FPS counter before and after Tweaker, GB freed by Cleaner, macro run in real time).
- Best data point: one indie developer's two TikToks with no link got 3,653 views, 45 installs (~1.2%) and 1 paid user. Installs were inferred, not tracked (HackerNoon). Evidence: self-reported, very weak, but it is the only views-to-installs number found.
- Show real numbers from a real PC, never invented ones.

**4. Video replies to "what app is this?" / "link?" comments.** Each reply becomes a new searchable post that answers buying intent. Evidence: none, logic only.

**5. "Search krypt cc on Google" / domain shown on screen.** Works before the bio link exists, and the "link in bio" reach-penalty myth doesn't matter here.
- WARC/TikTok research: 34% of TikTok search users start on TikTok and then switch to a search engine; 35% say content inspires them to search. Evidence: commissioned, moderate.
- Make sure krypt.cc ranks first for "krypt cc", "krypt tweaker", and so on.

**6. Pinned videos (3) and pinned comments as the landing page.**
- Pin: (a) "what is Krypt / which tool do I need", (b) the best before/after, (c) how to download safely (Windows SmartScreen warning explained, since the builds have no code signing).
- Pin a comment on every product video with one plain instruction.
- Evidence: none.

**7. Comparisons with paid tools** (vs paid optimisers or cleaners). Strong hook, but every price and claim must be sourced and accurate. TikTok's policies ban "false, deceptive or misleading claims" (Branded Content Policy, effective 2026-10-30). Evidence: none on conversion.

**8. Keyword DMs ("DM me FPS").**
- **Official auto-replies** (welcome message, keyword reply on an exact match, suggested questions, chat prompts) exist only for "Advanced Access" and Verified Business Accounts, not General accounts. Edits take about 1–5 days of review (TikTok Business Help Center, updated 2026-05). Evidence: official.
- **ManyChat** is an official TikTok partner, but its comment-to-DM trigger is beta and only in select regions. Keyword triggers work on DMs only (ManyChat community; InstantDM, 2026-08). Treat "comment KEYWORD, get an auto-DM" as unavailable for this account.
- **Age:** DMs are 16+ only, and 16–17 year olds default to "No one" (TikTok newsroom, 2026-01-16).
- Low expected yield until the account is verified.

**9. "Comment KEYWORD" CTAs.** With no auto-DM, someone has to send every DM by hand, and the agent sending DMs itself is an automation risk.
- TikTok makes content that "tries to manipulate people into… inflating likes or follows" ineligible for the For You feed (Integrity & Authenticity, effective 2026-09-24). A keyword-comment ask sits close to that line.
- Use it rarely, or not at all.

**10. Playlists.** Reportedly only for accounts with 10,000+ followers; no official criteria published (Shopify; Publer 2024). Later-stage tactic.

**11. TikTok Shop: not applicable.** Digital goods and software need invite-only "Virtual Goods" approval or aren't supported, and the sources conflict (third-party). Free downloads don't fit it anyway.

**12. Spark Ads / Promote: not needed.** Optional later, to boost a proven organic post. Paid ads for financial or prediction products fall under separate, stricter ad rules (below).

**"Link in bio" banned-word myth:** I found no evidence that TikTok cuts reach for the phrase. Social Media Today says there is "no evidence" TikTok restricts bio links. Old claims that comments containing "link/bio/website" get filtered were never confirmed (Inverse). The well-known test showing a penalty was on Instagram (Hootsuite), and Instagram's Mosseri denied any penalty. Evidence: weak either way. Plain phrasing is fine; avoid posting the same raw URL over and over.

### Disclosure and "promoting your own business"
- **Required:** TikTok requires the content disclosure setting when "promoting your own business, product, or service" (Community Guidelines, Regulated Goods & Commercial Activities, released 2026-08-25, effective 2026-09-24). Choose **"Your brand"**. "Branded content" is for paid third-party deals.
- **Labels:** "Commercial content" (own brand) or "Paid partnership".
- **Reach:** TikTok's internal study of ~2 million videos found disclosure does not change recommendations. Evidence: official, self-reported by TikTok.
- **Undisclosed content:** TikTok notifies you and gives 24 hours before the video becomes ineligible for the For You feed. Repeated failures can lead to posting limits or a ban (Ads Help Center, updated 2026-04).
- **Practice:** switch it on for any post whose purpose is pushing a download.

### Finance, crypto, prediction markets: the highest-risk area
- **Community Guidelines (effective 2026-09-24):** "We don't allow trading, marketing, or providing access to regulated, prohibited, or high-risk goods and services."
  - Gambling sits in this group. Gambling.com reports the gambling ban makes no legal-vs-illegal distinction.
  - Frauds & Scams bans promoting "fake investment offers, get-rich-quick schemes".
  - Prediction markets are **not named** in the organic rules (ISD Global). Whether Polymarket content counts as "gambling" is unresolved.
- **Ads:** prediction-market ads are reportedly allowed in 17 markets (US, Canada, UK and others), with sales-rep approval and trading rather than betting wording (SCCG, 2026-09-22, citing Gambling Insider). This is secondary; I found no TikTok text for it. The gambling ad policy (updated 2026-10) requires certification and legal markets.
- **Branded Content Policy (effective 2026-10-30):** prohibits "too-good-to-be-true financial offers" and "get-rich-quick" schemes, and restricts investment services.
- **Consequences for the bots:**
  - Never show profits, P&L screenshots or win rates, and never use "bet" or "gamble" wording.
  - Show the bot as a software tool: settings, risk limits, paper mode, "you can lose money".
  - **Flag:** the current line "AI trades for you, easy" leans toward "too-good-to-be-true". Consider dropping "easy". This is a judgment call for the user.
  - Use a **separate account** for the bots so a strike doesn't hit the PC tools account.
  - Using "free" for the Windows tools is fine. Never use "free to use" or "open source" for the bots.

### Off-platform links
I found no rule banning links in the bio. The documented limits are on which links can be added (no other social apps) and on spam behaviour (repeating the same link across many videos or comments).

  - Metricool found TikTok views down 31% and reach down 29% in early 2026, while posting volume rose about 80%. REPORTED. Expect low numbers early even on a clean account.

**2. Automation rules and the official API**

- **Community Guidelines, Integrity and Authenticity** (2026-08-25 version): "We strictly prohibit automation tools, scripts, or other tricks designed to bypass our systems." No accounts "that mislead or try to manipulate our platform", and no "services that artificially boost engagement or trick the recommendation system". Fake likes and follows get removed. Multiple accounts are allowed, "but not to deceive others", and a new account may not be used to get around a restriction or ban (TikTok may "ban additional or new accounts you create"). OFFICIAL: tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity/
- **ToS** (US, updated 2026-07-15), section 3.4: no scraping or extracting data "using any automated system or software, including automated 'bots'", and no "inauthentic commercial behaviors" such as spam accounts. Section 3.10: no using TikTok's generative AI features through automated software. OFFICIAL: tiktok.com/legal/page/us/terms-of-service/en
  - The August 2024 US ToS also banned automated scripts that "otherwise interact with the Services". I could not confirm that this wording is still in the 2026 text.
- **What this means for browser automation:** an AI driving a real Chrome window is not named anywhere. Agent-driven liking, following and commenting fits "automation tools, scripts" and "inauthentic engagement" closely enough that **TikTok can enforce against it.** Treat it as a ToS risk the user accepts, not a grey area.
- **Content Posting API** (the official route) is OFFICIAL, from developer docs updated 2026-08-04 (developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/content-sharing-guidelines and content-posting-api-reference-direct-post):
  - It is open to any developer app, not to individual creators. A creator authorises an app.

**3. What gets new accounts restricted, and how to check**

- **Strikes and bans:** the first violation gives a warning strike that does not count toward the tally. Later violations add strikes. Zero-tolerance violations mean an instant ban. Penalties range from feature restrictions (for example on comments, DMs or LIVE) to bans. OFFICIAL (newsroom 2024-04-22; CG Accounts & Features).
- **Not recommended ("ineligible for For You"):** repeatedly posting content that is allowed but not recommendable can make the whole account temporarily ineligible and harder to find in search. You are notified and can appeal. OFFICIAL. Content counts as ineligible if it is "unoriginal or reused material without anything new", "manipulate[s] people into... inflating likes or follows", or is moderately harmful misinformation. OFFICIAL (CG 2026). This matters for clip and repost styles: add real commentary or edits.
- **AI content:** creators must label realistic AI-generated or heavily edited content. Unlabelled content "may be removed, restricted, or labeled". C2PA content credentials (a provenance tag some AI tools embed) trigger an automatic label the creator cannot remove. OFFICIAL (CG 2026; support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content).
- **0-view jail on new accounts:** REPORTED widely, but by vendors and forums. The usual causes given are posting straight after signing up with no normal use, reused or watermarked content, a private account, and the "Suggest your account to others" setting being off. One forum post says TikTok Studio web uploads got 0 views while a phone upload got 87 (ANECDOTAL, blackhatworld). There is no controlled evidence that web uploads rank lower, and TikTok says nothing on it.
- **"You're tapping too fast" and "following too fast":** temporary blocks caused by fast likes, follows or comments, usually lasting about 24 hours (24 to 72 hours in some reports). REPORTED. TikTok publishes no duration.
- **Device and IP flags:** VPNs and datacenter IPs flagged by IP range, linked accounts banned together, fingerprinting. ANECDOTAL, and almost entirely from proxy and anti-detect vendors. What is OFFICIAL is that evading a ban with new accounts is prohibited. Practical reading: use a stable home or residential IP, and only a few accounts per IP.
- **How to check:**
  - "Account status" lists video violations. OFFICIAL.
  - "Account check" audits the account and its last 30 posts, and shows For You restrictions and comment or DM restrictions. OFFICIAL (newsroom 2024-04-22). Its path is Profile > menu > TikTok Studio > Account check, and it may exist only in the mobile app (ANECDOTAL). I found no confirmed web page for it.
  - Individual posts show "ineligible for For You" notices with an appeal option once analytics are on. REPORTED.

**4. Does a warm-up help?**

- **For:** vendors and forums agree that posting immediately through a tool with no normal use leads to flags and 0 views (oneupapp help, tokportal, influencermarketinghub). The trust and velocity logic is plausible.
- **Against:** TikTok has never mentioned warm-ups. Every schedule out there comes from someone selling tools. One vendor admits its checks are "not platform-approved warm-up schedules". TikTok says it judges content video by video, and many creators report first videos doing well.
- **Verdict:** a short, light warm-up is cheap insurance. A long one (30 days) is not proven.
- **Suggested 7 days for a brand-new account:**
  - **Day 1:** finish the profile (photo, bio, no link yet). Watch 20 to 30 minutes of niche content (search the niche, watch videos to the end). 5 likes, no follows.
  - **Day 2:** 25 to 30 minutes, 8 likes, 1 to 2 follows.
  - **Day 3:** 30 minutes, 10 likes, 2 to 3 follows, 1 short comment.
  - **Day 4:** same, plus 2 comments. Make the account Creator or Business if you need scheduling.
  - **Day 5:** first video (original, labelled as AI if realistic), then stay about 15 minutes to engage. Check that it gets more than 0 views within 24 hours.
  - **Days 6 and 7:** 1 video a day if day 5 got views; if not, wait 48 hours. Up to 15 likes, 5 follows, 3 comments. Reply to every comment on your own videos.
- **An already-active account** needs no warm-up. Increase volume about 25 to 50% a week, and only while views on your own posts stay normal.

**5. Safe daily volumes (unofficial, my synthesis of the sources above)**

| Account age | Videos | Likes | Follows | Comments | Cold DMs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1-3 | 0 | 5-10 | 0-3 | 0-1 | 0 |
| Days 4-7 | 1 (from day 5) | 10-25 | 3-5 | 2-3 | 0 |
| Days 8-14 | 1-2 | 25-40 | 5-10 | 3-5 | 0 (replies only) |
| Days 15-30 | 1-3 | 40-60 | 10-15 | 5-10 | 0-2 |
| 30+ days | 1-3 (4 at most) | 60-100 | 15-25 | 10-15 | 2-5 |

Every number is far below the reported hard limits (200 follows, 500+ likes). The real risk is the pattern (bursts, identical comments, no watching), not the totals.

**On the app's current defaults:**
- **Days 1-3** (30 min, 10 likes, 2 follows): fine.
- **Days 4-7** (25 likes, 5 follows, 2 comments, 1 video): fine. Put the first video on day 5, not day 4.
- **Days 8-14** (40 likes, 10 follows, 5 comments, 2 videos): fine, but make the second video conditional on the first ones getting views.
- **After day 14** (60 likes, 15 follows, 10 comments, 3 videos): fine for engagement. 3 videos a day from desktop is at the top of the range, so I'd default to 2 and allow 3 only once reach is proven.
- **Watch time:** the defaults are slightly light on watching. Keep at least 20 to 30 minutes of watching a day after week 1, with roughly one like per two to three minutes watched.
- **DMs:** there is no DM default. I recommend no cold DMs for the first 14 days.

**6. Hard rules for an AI agent running a TikTok account in a browser**

1. One account per Chrome profile, and a few accounts at most per home IP. No datacenter IPs or VPN hopping. Never create a new account after a ban or restriction (this is explicitly banned).
2. Before posting, open the Account status and Account check pages (or the post notices). If any restriction or "ineligible" flag shows, stop engaging and posting for 48 hours and tell the user.
3. On any "tapping too fast", "following too fast", captcha or action-blocked message, stop that action for 24 hours, and the whole session if it repeats. Never retry in a loop.
4. Watch before acting: view each video for a while before liking, following or commenting. Spread actions randomly across the session, with no bursts and no fixed intervals.
5. Comments must be short, specific and human-sounding, never repeated, and never contain links, "follow me" or "check my profile". At most 2 per thread.
6. Post only original or clearly transformed content. Label realistic AI visuals or voices with TikTok's AI-generated toggle. Never re-upload other people's clips without a real edit, and no watermarks from other apps.
7. No engagement bait ("like for part 2", follow-for-follow), and no bought or exchanged engagement.
8. No cold DMs before day 14, a handful a day after that, never the same text twice, and no links in a first message. Respect the one-request rule.
9. Increase volume only when your own posts keep getting views. On a 0-view streak, slow down rather than speed up.
10. Be honest with the user: posting from a browser through an agent sits outside TikTok's automation rules. The only sanctioned posting route is an audited Content Posting API app, and there is no sanctioned route for likes, follows, comments or DMs.

### Patterns that repeat across the winners

1. **One series title, numbered parts.** "Powerful Websites… (Part 797)". Viewers know what they'll get, and it's easy to mass-produce.
2. **Very short.** 7–20 seconds for website tips and odds clips. Only longer explainers run 30–60 seconds.
3. **Show the result in second one.** The site or setting is on screen right away; there's no intro.
4. **One benefit line in the caption,** written in plain user terms ("learn almost anything"), not feature lists.
5. **High posting volume.** About 1–5 posts a day from the winners. Slower accounts plateau.
6. **Bio does the selling, not the video.** Usually "Download X below", "Search 'X' on iOS", or a /tiktok or referral link. Comment-keyword funnels mostly show up with info-product sellers.
7. **App accounts copy a winning hook.** Once a slideshow hook works, they reuse it with similar images. Comments asking "what app?" do the promotion.
8. **Ride news and sports.** The prediction-market accounts grew by attaching odds to whatever is trending, not by showing trades.
9. **A brand account plus outside creators.** The app's own account captures search traffic; influencer and UGC posts create the reach.
10. **General lists flatten out.** @aisavvy and @setupspawn both slowed. A narrow, repeatable angle keeps growing.

### What has stalled or been banned

- **Command-paste "activation" videos:** AI-made TikToks telling viewers to paste PowerShell commands to activate Windows, Office or Spotify spread the Vidar, StealC and Aura password stealers. One video got about 500K views, and the accounts were removed (Trend Micro 2025; Security Affairs). Never show a "paste this into PowerShell" step.
- **Scareware optimizers:** in 2024 the FTC fined Restoro and Reimage $26M over fake "your PC is infected" scans.
- **Fake FPS boosters:** paid tweak tools showed small or negative gains in independent testing. "Aim tweaks" and "bloom reducers" were called scams in the same test, and "cracked" optimizer downloads are a known malware route.
- **Fake winnings:** a WSJ investigation (June 2026) found Polymarket paid creators for 1,100+ videos showing fake bets, some on fake look-alike Polymarket sites. More than 100 videos claimed about $900K in wins on bets that would really have lost $166K. "Free money" was a recurring line, and creators were told not to disclose payment. The FTC endorsement rules and the commodities regulator (CFTC) are possible exposure.
- **Kalshi's TikTok push:** Kalshi's TikTok ads drew Media Matters criticism and state gambling regulators.
- **"Passive income" AI trading bots:** a common scam pattern (Gizmodo/Techlicious, Group-IB).
- **TikTok policy:** TikTok prohibits financial services and crypto in branded content and removes get-rich-quick and investment-scheme content.
- **AI-made videos:** since Nov 2025 viewers can turn AI-made videos down with a slider in their feed settings. Realistic AI voices and faces need a label, and TikTok checks for unlabeled AI media.
